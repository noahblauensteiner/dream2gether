# 🎃 October: Chestnuts, Storms & a Very Brave Spider

*Alex & Sam · October 2026 · 12 favourite moments from 16 nights*

## Journal

October started with wet leaves and office shoes on the riverside path. It ended with a pumpkin
spider winning a costume contest. In between we did the things we love most:

- **eating:** hot chestnuts on the market bench, and a ramen that took six hours and was worth
  every minute;
- **staying in:** a storm we watched with all the lights off and two mugs of ginger tea;
- **being a bit ridiculous:** Alex's one-point victory, and Pepper the dog's change of heart.

It was also a month of firsts. Alex topped a yellow route at our very first bouldering session,
which ticked off a dream from the summer. Together we cooked something neither of us thought we
could. And Grandma Rosi gave us a brand-new family legend about a dance and a stolen bicycle.

If October had a sound, it would be counting the seconds between lightning and thunder. If it
had a taste, it would be chestnuts that are slightly too hot.
